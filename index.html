Privacy Policy

Lovely Finds Publisher
Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. About this application

Lovely Finds Publisher is a private application intended to help the owner manage content for the Lovely Finds Pinterest account. Its planned features include connecting to a Pinterest Business account, viewing the account's boards, and preparing Pinterest Pins with images, titles, descriptions and destination links.

2. Information the application may access

Only after the account owner authorises access through Pinterest, the application may access information permitted by the permissions granted to it. Depending on the permissions and features enabled, this may include basic account information, board names and identifiers, and Pin information needed to create or manage Pins.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the Pinterest content-management features requested by the account owner, such as selecting a board and preparing or publishing Pins. The intended workflow requires the owner to review and approve each Pin before publication. Pinterest account information is not used to build advertising profiles or sold to third parties.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Lovely Finds does not sell personal information. Information accessed through Pinterest is not shared with third parties for their independent advertising or marketing. Information may be processed by Pinterest and by technical service providers strictly as needed to operate the application. Those providers should receive only the information necessary for their role.

5. Storage and security

Authentication credentials and any account information retained by the application will be protected using reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Access tokens and other secrets will not be placed in public source code or intentionally disclosed. No security measure can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention, deletion and revoking access

Information will be retained only for as long as needed to provide the application features or meet applicable obligations. The account owner may revoke the application's access through Pinterest account settings. To request deletion of information stored by the application, contact the email below. Requests will be handled within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Pinterest and third-party services

The application uses or is intended to use the Pinterest API and is subject to Pinterest's terms and developer policies. Pinterest handles information in accordance with its own privacy policy. Lovely Finds is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application or its data practices change. The current version will be published at this address with the updated date.
