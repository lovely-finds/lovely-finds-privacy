Privacy Policy

Lovely Finds Social Publisher
Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. About this application

Lovely Finds Social Publisher is a private application developed by Lovely Finds (Cantinho dos Achados) to support the management of the brand's Pinterest content.

The application is intended for use by the owner of the Lovely Finds Pinterest account. Its planned features include connecting to an authorised Pinterest Business account, selecting boards, preparing Pins with images, titles, descriptions and destination links, and publishing content following the account owner's instructions.

Pinterest integration and publishing features are currently under development and are not yet active. The application does not currently access Pinterest account information through the Pinterest API.

2. Information we may access

When Pinterest integration becomes available, the application may request access to information permitted by the permissions explicitly granted by the account owner through Pinterest's authorisation process.

Depending on the approved permissions and implemented features, this may include:

  • Basic Pinterest account information.
  • Board names and identifiers.
  • Pin information required for the requested content-management operations.

The application will only request information necessary to provide its stated features.

We do not request Pinterest passwords, session cookies or other login credentials.

3. How information is used

Information obtained through Pinterest will only be used to provide the content-management features requested by the account owner.

These purposes may include:

  • Identifying the authorised Pinterest account.
  • Retrieving available boards when needed.
  • Preparing Pins using content supplied by the account owner.
  • Carrying out publishing actions specifically selected and authorised by the account owner.

The application will not use Pinterest information to create advertising profiles, train artificial intelligence models, sell data or provide independent advertising services.

4. Data storage and retention

We follow Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and API Terms regarding the handling of information obtained through Pinterest.

Pinterest information accessed through its API will not be persistently stored unless Pinterest has expressly authorised such storage in writing or an applicable exception under its policies applies.

When information is needed to provide an application feature, the application will request it through the Pinterest API as required.

Any authentication credentials or access tokens used by the application will be handled according to the security requirements applicable to the implemented integration.

The technical implementation and storage arrangements for Pinterest authentication credentials are still being finalised. Pinterest integration will not be activated until appropriate security and data-handling measures have been implemented.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Lovely Finds does not sell or rent personal information.

Information obtained through Pinterest will not be sold, shared with advertising networks or disclosed to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Technical service providers may process information only when necessary to operate the application, subject to appropriate security and confidentiality safeguards and applicable Pinterest requirements.

6. Security

We intend to implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

These measures will include appropriate protection of authentication credentials and access tokens, restricted access to application infrastructure and secure communication channels.

The application will not be activated for Pinterest operations until the relevant security controls have been implemented.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

7. Revoking access and deleting information

The account owner may revoke the application's access through the relevant Pinterest account settings.

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning information handled by Lovely Finds, the account owner may contact us using the email address provided below.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws and Pinterest's requirements.

Any Pinterest information held by the application will be handled in accordance with Pinterest's applicable deletion and data-retention requirements.

8. Third-party services

The application is intended to integrate with the Pinterest API.

Pinterest processes information under its own Terms and Privacy Policy: Pinterest Privacy Policy.

Use of the Pinterest API is subject to Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and Developer and API Terms of Service.

Lovely Finds is an independent brand and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Pinterest.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, integrations or data-handling practices change.

If material changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this same address with a revised update date.

The policy will be reviewed before new Pinterest integration features are activated to ensure that it accurately describes the application's actual operation.
